FAQ

SEO services FAQs

What does an SEO audit include?

A technical review of crawling, indexing and site structure, an on-page review of titles, headings, content and internal links, a keyword and intent gap review, and a backlink profile check with a prioritized action list.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes can be picked up within weeks once pages are recrawled. Content and authority work usually needs several months, depending on competition, the site's history and how consistently the plan is carried out.

Do you build backlinks?

Yes. Backlink campaigns are a core part of my agency role: finding relevant websites, outreach and relationship building, monitoring in Ahrefs and SEMrush, and auditing for potentially harmful links.

Can you work with my developer and content writer?

Yes. Most of my work is collaborative. I share clear SEO requirements with writers, designers and developers and check that changes are implemented correctly.
