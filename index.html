About Danyal

Ranking for a keyword is not enough. Google has to understand the page.

I'm Danyal Zahoor Malik, an SEO Specialist from Islamabad with more than three years of experience inside a digital marketing agency. I work on SEO projects for local Pakistani businesses and international clients, aligning every task with the website's goals and the intent behind each search.

My day-to-day work moves between technical checks, on-page optimization, semantic content planning and link building. I collaborate closely with content writers, designers and developers so that SEO decisions actually make it onto the live site.

I build pages around entities, not just strings of keywords. Keywords show how people search; entities tell Google what a page is and what it knows. Good SEO needs both.

  • Search intent first: understanding what the searcher wants to learn, compare or buy.
  • Technical foundation: making sites easy to crawl, index and navigate.
  • Semantic structure: a clear central entity, related entities and topical depth.
  • Trusted links: relevant backlinks and regular audits to keep the profile healthy.

SEO Insights

What I share on LinkedIn.

Short, practical notes on how search is changing.

Entity SEO

From strings to things

A keyword is a string of letters; an entity is a real thing with facts and connections. My method: one central entity per page, mention the entities a real expert would, place keywords naturally in headings and answers, and back it up with consistent naming, descriptive internal links and schema using about, mentions and sameAs.

Core Updates

Don't panic mid-rollout

During Google's November 2026 core update my advice was simple: wait for the rollout to finish before big changes, check your data for reporting issues first, remember that being cited in AI Mode matters as much as being clicked, and double down on E-E-A-T, topical authority and clear intent.

What is semantic SEO?
