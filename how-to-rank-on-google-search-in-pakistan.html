How to Rank on Google Search in Pakistan: A Practical Guide

A step-by-step SEO guide for Pakistani websites, from technical basics to semantic content, local SEO and backlinks.

Ranking on Google in Pakistan is not about tricks. It comes down to three things: Google must be able to reach your pages, understand them, and trust them more than the alternatives. Here is how to work on each.

1. Start with Google Search Console

Verify your site in Google Search Console and submit your XML sitemap. It shows which pages are indexed, which queries bring impressions, and which technical problems Google has found. Every decision after this should use its data.

2. Fix the technical foundation

Check that important pages return a 200 status, are not blocked in robots.txt, are not marked noindex and have a correct canonical tag. Fix broken links and redirect chains, keep URLs short and descriptive, and make sure the site works well on mobile, since most Pakistani users browse on phones, often on slower connections.

3. Research keywords and search intent

Use Ahrefs, SEMrush or Google's own suggestions to find what people actually type. Pakistani searches mix English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, and often include a city. For every keyword, look at the current top results and ask: does Google show guides, service pages, product pages or local listings? Build the page type that matches.

4. Build pages around entities, not just keywords

A keyword is a string of letters; an entity is a real thing: a treatment, a service, a product, a place. Choose one central entity per page, then cover the attributes and related entities a real expert would mention: cost factors, duration, who it suits, risks, the professionals involved and the location. This is semantic SEO, and it is how Google and AI systems understand what you know.

5. Optimize on-page elements

  • Title tag: the main topic and location, written naturally, under about 60 characters.
  • Meta description: a clear reason to click.
  • One H1 and a logical H2/H3 structure that follows the questions people ask.
  • Answer first: put the direct answer in the first sentence of each section.
  • Images: compressed, with descriptive alt text.

6. Use internal links deliberately

Connect related pages with descriptive anchor text, for example a condition page linking to its treatment page and the doctor's profile. Internal links help Google discover pages, understand topical relationships and pass authority to your most important pages.

7. Win local SEO in your city

Create and complete a Google Business Profile with the correct category, address, phone, hours and photos. Keep your business name, address and phone consistent across your site and directories. Build dedicated pages for each city or area you serve, with genuinely local details, and ask happy customers for reviews.

8. Earn relevant backlinks

Links from relevant, real websites remain a strong trust signal. Focus on industry publications, local business associations, partners, suppliers and quality guest contributions. Avoid buying bulk links from unrelated sites, and audit your backlink profile regularly for spammy or harmful links.

Quality over quantity: after Google's recent spam updates, a few relevant links and tightly focused, genuinely useful pages outperform mass-produced content.

9. Prepare for AI search

Google AI Overviews and AI Mode summarize answers from pages they trust and understand. Help them with clear entities, answer-first sections, consistent naming across your site, author and business information (E-E-A-T), and structured data such as Organization, Person, LocalBusiness and FAQ schema.

10. Measure and refine

Review Search Console every month: impressions, clicks, average position and indexing. Update pages that get impressions but few clicks, expand pages that rank on page two, and stay calm during core updates. Wait for the rollout to finish before making big changes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to rank on Google in Pakistan?

New pages in low-competition niches can rank within weeks; competitive keywords usually take several months of consistent technical, content and link work.

Is SEO free?

Ranking organically has no cost per click, but it takes time, skills and usually some investment in content and outreach.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

For any business serving customers in a specific city, yes. It is essential for Maps and local results.

Should I write content in Urdu or English?

Match how your audience searches. Many Pakistani searches use English or Roman Urdu; check real queries in Search Console and keyword tools.
