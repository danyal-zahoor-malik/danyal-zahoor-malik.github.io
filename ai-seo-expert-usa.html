Market insight

Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and search visibility

AI Overviews and AI Mode do not count how many times a keyword appears. They look for clear entities, how those entities connect, and sources that explain a topic reliably. Being cited in an AI answer is becoming as valuable as a classic blue-link click.

Keywords still matter. They show how people search and which questions to answer. Entities show what your business is and what it knows. The best results come from using both together.

For US businesses with state- or city-level intent, the same principle applies locally: clear location entities, real addresses and service details help both Google and AI tools match you to the right searches.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the practice of structuring content and websites so that AI-powered search features and assistants can understand, trust and cite them, alongside traditional Google rankings.

Is AI SEO different from normal SEO?

It builds on the same foundations (technical health, relevance and authority) with extra focus on entities, direct answers and structured data.

Can you work with US time zones?

Do keywords still matter?

Yes. Keywords show demand and phrasing; entities and structure make your page the clear answer.
