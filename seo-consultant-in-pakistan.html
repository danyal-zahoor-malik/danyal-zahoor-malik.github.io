When does a business need an SEO consultant?

Many businesses already have a website and a marketing team, but organic traffic is flat or falling. A consultant brings an outside view: checking whether the problem is technical, content-related, competitive or caused by an algorithm update.

Common signs you need one: pages that are not indexed, rankings that dropped after a core update, a new website launch or migration, content that gets published but never ranks, or a backlink profile you are unsure about.

The goal of consulting is not a long report. It is a short list of the right actions, in the right order, with clear owners.

SituationWhat I review first
Traffic dropIndexing, update timing, lost pages and links
New websiteStructure, URLs, internal links, redirects
Content not rankingIntent match, entity coverage, competition
Unsure about linksBacklink audit for harmful or irrelevant links

What is the difference between an SEO consultant and an SEO executive?

A consultant diagnoses problems and builds the plan; an executive carries out tasks. I do both, so plans stay realistic and can be executed.

Can you train my in-house team?

Yes. I can share processes for on-page optimization, semantic briefs and backlink checks with your writers and developers.
